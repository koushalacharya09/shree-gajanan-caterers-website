Why destination weddings need different catering

Destination wedding caterers in and around Jaipur deal with more than a single banquet dinner. Guests may arrive from other cities, meals stretch across welcome dinners and farewell brunches, and kitchens often sit far from a permanent commercial setup. Power, water, storage and early load-in become part of the menu conversation.

Shree Gajanan Caterers plans Rajasthani and North Indian spreads for celebrations that want a clear sense of place — without turning every plate into a tourist gimmick. We focus on portion reliability, dietary clarity and service that still feels warm when the schedule slips.

  • Rajasthani signatures such as Dal Baati where they fit the story
  • Vegetarian and Jain-friendly lines for mixed guest lists
  • Outdoor kitchen placement and buffet circulation planning
  • Staffing for resort, farmhouse and heritage venues
