What wedding catering includes

A Jaipur wedding is rarely a single meal. Guests move between rituals, photographs and hospitality — so catering has to stay flexible without feeling chaotic.

Shree Gajanan Caterers helps you plan:

  • Reception and dinner menus sized to guest count
  • Live counters and chaat stations for flow between courses
  • Vegetarian and Jain-friendly lines when families need them
  • Rajasthani specialties for destination or traditional functions
  • Staffing and service pacing matched to the venue
