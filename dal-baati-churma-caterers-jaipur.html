Why Dal Baati needs specialised catering

Dal Baati Churma looks simple on a plate and is demanding at scale. Baati must stay textured, dal must stay hot and churma must be finished so it does not dry out before the last seating. Caterers who treat it as a side dish often disappoint; hosts who treat it as the centrepiece get the hospitality people talk about.

Shree Gajanan Caterers plans Dal Baati as a signature line for Rajasthani weddings, religious Sawamani events and large family functions. We discuss guest count early so cooking batches, serving style and supporting sabzi lines stay aligned.
