Family-led hospitality for weddings, prasadi gatherings and celebrations where food must feel like home — and run like an event.

Who we are

Shree Gajanan Caterers is a Jaipur-based, family-led catering brand (also known locally alongside Shree Siddhivinayak Caterers). From Subhash Chowk in the Pink City, we plan and serve food for weddings, religious ceremonies, corporate hospitality and outdoor celebrations.

We are not a generic “event package” vendor. Our strength is regional food culture — Rajasthani menus, Dal Baati Churma, Sawamani Prashadi — delivered with the discipline large guest lists demand.

Our philosophy

  • Taste and hygiene come before decorative language
  • Service should stay calm when guest flow is busy
  • Vegetarian and Jain-friendly needs are planned early
  • Quotes should help you decide — not confuse you with vague luxury words

What we specialise in

Wedding catering, destination wedding logistics in Rajasthan, Rajasthani cuisine, Sawamani Prashadi, live food counters, corporate gatherings and family celebrations across Jaipur.
