A Jaipur catering company built on trust and taste.

Shree Gajanan Caterers works from Pink City with a simple brief: plan food that fits the occasion, then serve it with composure.

Who we are

Shree Gajanan Caterers (also known locally alongside Shree Siddhivinayak Caterers) is a catering and hospitality team based at Subhash Chowk, Jaipur. We focus on weddings, family celebrations, corporate gatherings and outdoor functions across the city.

Our work sits at the intersection of Rajasthani food culture and modern event service — menus guests recognise, executed with the discipline large occasions demand.

How we think about hospitality

  • Food quality and hygiene come before decoration language
  • Service should feel calm even when guest flow is busy
  • Dietary needs (vegetarian, Jain-friendly) are planned early, not patched later
  • Quotes should be clear enough to decide — not inflated with vague luxury adjectives

Where we work

Primary service area: Jaipur. Destination events elsewhere in Rajasthan are discussed case-by-case based on logistics.
