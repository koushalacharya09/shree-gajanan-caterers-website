# Formspree setup (email lead notifications)

1. Create a free form at https://formspree.io
2. Copy your form endpoint (looks like `https://formspree.io/f/xxxxxxxx`)
3. In `js/main.js`, replace:

   `const FORMSPREE_ENDPOINT = 'https://formspree.io/f/REPLACE_ME';`

   with your real endpoint.

Until then, enquiries still open WhatsApp — email notify is skipped safely.
