What corporate catering needs to get right

A business event rarely runs on a wedding timeline. Guests arrive in waves, sessions overrun, and food has to appear when the agenda says it should — not when the kitchen hopes it will.

Shree Gajanan Caterers plans corporate catering in Jaipur around that reality: clear menu choices, predictable service windows and staffing that stays calm when the schedule shifts.

Events we support

  • Conference and seminar lunches or high-tea breaks
  • Office celebrations, festival gatherings and team milestones
  • Client dinners, appreciation evenings and small receptions
  • Training days, workshops and off-site meetings
  • Product or brand hospitality with standing or seated service
  • Board meetings and compact executive meals

How we structure a corporate menu

We start with guest count, dietary mix and how long people will be eating. A thirty-minute tea break needs a different layout than a seated lunch after a keynote.

Typical building blocks include:

  • Vegetarian North Indian spreads with optional Rajasthani specialties
  • Live counters for chaat or dessert when you want movement between sessions
  • Jain and onion/garlic-free lines when requested early — see vegetarian catering
  • Tea, coffee and refreshment stations coordinated with break times
  • Buffet or plated service depending on venue flow

What to share for a useful quote

Corporate catering cost depends on headcount, menu depth, service style and venue constraints. A short brief helps more than a generic package label:

  • Date, approximate guest count and event type
  • Venue name or area (and whether it is indoor or outdoor)
  • Meal windows — breakfast, tea, lunch, dinner or multiple breaks
  • Dietary notes: vegetarian-only, Jain, allergies
  • Whether you need live counters, dessert stations or simple plated service

Send those details through enquire or WhatsApp and we will respond with a practical direction — not a one-size menu.
