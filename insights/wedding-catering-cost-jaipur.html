How much does wedding catering cost in Jaipur?

Hosts ask for a single number. In practice, Jaipur wedding catering quotes move with menu depth, guest count, venue constraints and service format.

What usually drives the quote

  • Guest count — kitchen scale, staffing and crockery requirements rise with headcount.
  • Menu composition — Rajasthani specialty lines, live counters and dessert stations change cost structure.
  • Venue type — banquet halls and outdoor venues need different setup and logistics.
  • Dietary lines — separate Jain or onion/garlic-free kitchens add planning complexity.
  • Service duration — multi-function days need staggered menus and longer staffing windows.

Typical conversation ranges

Across Jaipur, families often discuss per-plate budgets in broad bands — from simpler celebration menus to richer multi-course wedding spreads. Treat published internet ranges as orientation only; your venue and guest list decide the real figure.

What to share for a useful quote

  1. Wedding date (or month) and city area / venue name
  2. Approximate guest count
  3. Functions needed (reception only vs multi-day)
  4. Vegetarian / Jain requirements
  5. Any must-have dishes (for example Dal Bati Churma)
