How to choose a wedding caterer in Jaipur

The right caterer is less about slogans and more about whether they understand your guest list, venue and service timing.

1. Start with the guest experience

Ask how they pace service for 150 versus 800 guests. Crowding at counters and cold food are usually planning problems, not kitchen accidents.

2. Confirm dietary clarity

If your family needs Jain-friendly or onion/garlic-free lines, ask how those kitchens are separated and labelled. Ambiguity here creates day-of stress.

3. Match cuisine to the celebration

Jaipur weddings often want a Rajasthani signature dish alongside broader North Indian spreads. Confirm whether Dal Bati, live counters or chaat stations are routine for them — see Rajasthani catering.

4. Visit the venue conversation early

Outdoor and heritage venues need kitchen placement, power and water planning. If your celebration is open-air, read outdoor catering and ask for a site walkthrough plan.

5. Ask for a clear inclusions list

Staffing, crockery, buffet equipment, live-counter fuel and cleanup should be named. Vague “package” language is hard to compare.
