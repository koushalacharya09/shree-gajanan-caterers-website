Outdoor catering is a site plan first

A lawn or farmhouse looks open until you place cooking, plating, buffet lines and waste. Then the space becomes a puzzle: where heat goes, where guests walk, and how food stays consistent from the first plate to the last.

Shree Gajanan Caterers treats outdoor catering in Jaipur as menu plus site — not decoration alone.

Venues we commonly support

  • Private gardens and residential lawns
  • Farmhouses and resort outdoor spaces
  • Banquet lawns attached to halls
  • Terrace and courtyard celebrations when access allows
  • Destination outdoor sites across Rajasthan when travel is planned early

Logistics we discuss before the menu is final

  • Kitchen and prep placement — distance from buffet, wind, safety and venue rules
  • Power and water — live counters, chafing and lighting all depend on reliable supply
  • Guest circulation — so queues do not block rituals, photos or entry paths
  • Weather contingencies — shade, cover and backup timing for Jaipur seasons
  • Access for vehicles and staff — load-in times matter on farmhouse roads
  • Waste and hygiene — outdoor service still needs clean, disciplined back-of-house

Menus that work outdoors

Outdoor service favors dishes that hold temperature and travel short distances well. Live counters — chaat, baati, dessert — can work when power and space are confirmed.
