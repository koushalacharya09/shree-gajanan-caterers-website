Vegetarian catering that is planned, not improvised

Many Jaipur celebrations are vegetarian by default. The harder work is when families need Jain lines, onion/garlic-free cooking, or separate counters so guests are not left guessing what is safe.

Shree Gajanan Caterers builds pure vegetarian catering around those requests — stated early, written into the menu, and followed through staffing and plating.

What we can put on the table

  • Full pure vegetarian North Indian buffets and seated meals
  • Jain-friendly menus when requested at the planning stage
  • Onion- and garlic-free preparations for specific guest groups or full events
  • Rajasthani vegetarian specialties — Dal Bati, Ker Sangri and thali formats via Rajasthani catering
  • Live chaat, dessert and beverage stations that stay within vegetarian rules
  • Sweets and festive items coordinated with the meal, not added as an afterthought

Jain and onion/garlic-free service

These lines need more than a verbal note on the day. We discuss which dishes are fully Jain, which are onion/garlic-free but not Jain, and how counters are labelled so guests can move confidently.

When only part of the guest list needs a restricted line, we plan portioning and placement so those guests are not left with a thin “special” plate while everyone else has a full spread.

Where vegetarian catering fits

  • Wedding catering — receptions and multi-function days with mixed family preferences
  • Event catering — birthdays, engagements, housewarmings and social gatherings
  • Corporate catering — office and conference meals with clear dietary options
  • Outdoor catering — garden and farmhouse vegetarian service with site logistics

Kitchen discipline guests notice

Vegetarian trust is built in prep: separate handling where required, clear labelling on the buffet, and staff who can answer simple questions without guessing. We prefer those answers to be decided in planning, not invented at the counter.

How to brief us

Tell us whether the entire event is pure vegetarian, whether Jain or onion/garlic-free lines are required, and approximate guest counts for each. Add venue type and meal timing so we can size counters correctly.
